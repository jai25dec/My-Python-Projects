## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyses 200K+ Zomato restaurant records across multiple Indian cities to uncover pricing patterns, cuisine popularity, rating distributions, and market gaps — the kind of analysis used in restaurant chain expansion, franchise territory planning, and competitive pricing strategy.

---

## 🎯 Objective

Clean and analyse a large, messy multi-city restaurant dataset to surface actionable insights on cuisine trends, customer ratings, and pricing — supporting market entry and competitive positioning decisions.

---

## 📊 Dataset

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| Records | 200,000+ |
| Cities | 5+ major Indian cities |
| Key Fields | restaurant_name, cuisine, city, price_for_two, rating, votes, online_order, book_table, location |

---

## 🔍 Key Analysis

### Data Cleaning (Critical at 200K+ Scale)
- **Missing value handling** — identified and imputed nulls across rating, price, and cuisine fields
- **Duplicate removal** — detected and dropped duplicate restaurant listings
- **Type enforcement** — fixed mixed-type columns (price stored as string, ratings as object)
- **Outlier treatment** — capped extreme price values and zero-vote ratings
- **Achieved 98%+ data quality** before any analysis

### EDA & Insights (7+)
| Analysis | Business Question |
|----------|-------------------|
| Cuisine frequency by city | Which cuisines dominate each market? |
| Price band distribution | What price segments are most competitive? |
| Rating vs. price scatter | Do expensive restaurants rate higher? |
| Online order adoption | Which restaurant types enable online ordering? |
| Vote count vs. rating | Does popularity correlate with quality? |
| City-wise average pricing | How do price levels differ across cities? |
| Cuisine vs. average rating | Which cuisines consistently rate highest? |

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- **North Indian and Chinese** cuisines dominate across all 5 cities — highest listing volume
- **Cafes and Fast Food** showed the highest online order adoption rates
- A **price-rating sweet spot** exists in the ₹300–₹600 range — highest density of 4.0+ rated restaurants
- **Bangalore** had the most competitive restaurant density — highest average votes per listing
- Restaurants offering **table booking** rated on average 0.4 points higher than those that don't
- Several cities showed **underserved cuisine categories** — whitespace for new entrants
